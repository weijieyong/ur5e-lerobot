# Clamp and slide on Workspace boundaries inside UR5e.send_action

We enforce the five-face Cartesian Workspace (`UR5E_Z_MIN_M` and optional `UR5E_{X,Y}_{MIN,MAX}_M`) by default in `UR5e.send_action()` by clamping out-of-bounds targets onto the violated face(s) via FK → clip → IK (re-verifying both `max_joint_step_rad` and FK, and falling back to holding `q_meas` if IK fails) rather than aborting the active rollout episode. Because SpaceMouse demonstrations themselves clamp and slide along `UR5E_Z_MIN_M`, policy trajectories legitimately sit on the floor plane during table-height grasps where 1–3 mm diffusion noise would constantly false-abort episodes; clamping limits TCP targets at those faces while allowing normal grasps and keeping the supervising operator in charge of ending stuck trials. Neutral/tilt bounds remain on SpaceMouse teleop only, `go_home()` remains exempt, and rollout requires `UR5E_Z_MIN_M` only while Workspace enforcement is enabled. Set `ROLLOUT_ENFORCE_WORKSPACE=false` in `config/local/machine.env` to disable it for rollouts without erasing saved bounds; joint-step limiting and controller checks remain active. These TCP bounds do not establish whole-arm or swept-path safety.
